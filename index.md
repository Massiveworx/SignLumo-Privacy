SignLumo Privacy Statement
About this statement
SignLumo is provided by Massiveworx. This statement applies to the current offline version of SignLumo for iPhone and iPad. Last updated: November 7, 2026.

Information you enter
SignLumo saves the sign text, display preferences, pickup records (including passenger names, dates, times, optional flight numbers and meeting locations), VIP public text, and the company logo you choose. These items are used to create signs and organize your pickups.

Storage and collection
This version processes and saves your app content on your device. SignLumo does not send passenger names, pickup records, or logos to Massiveworx or a flight-data provider. It has no account system, advertising SDK, third-party analytics SDK, or live flight-tracking connection. Massiveworx does not sell or share this app content.

Photos and Files
When you import a logo, SignLumo uses the system Photos or Files picker to read the item you select and save a resized copy in the app. It does not scan your photo library. If you select an item stored in iCloud or another file service, that service may download it under its own privacy practices.

Public signs and VIP privacy
Anything shown on a pickup sign can be seen, photographed, or recorded by people nearby. VIP privacy replaces the passenger name on the sign with the public text you enter, such as initials, a company name, or a pickup code. The full name remains visible in the driver’s pickup list and editor. VIP privacy does not add an app lock or encrypt the pickup list. Use only passenger information and company logos you are authorized to use.

Retention and deletion
Saved information remains until you change or delete it. Swipe to delete a pickup in the pickup list; marking it Complete does not delete it. Clear the guest-name and VIP public-text fields separately in the editor, because deleting a pickup does not clear a previously loaded sign. Tap Remove in Company Logo to delete the app’s saved logo copy; the original in Photos or Files is unchanged. Deleting SignLumo using Delete App removes its local app data; Offload App may retain it. Device or cloud backup copies may remain under your backup settings.

Apple services and backups
SignLumo does not provide its own cloud sync. Your device settings may include app data in Apple or computer backups and restore it later. Apple handles App Store payments and may provide purchase reports, app analytics, or diagnostics under its own settings and privacy policy. These Apple services are separate from the passenger content stored by SignLumo.

Support and contact
For privacy questions, contact Massiveworx at 855-922-1100. If you contact support, Massiveworx receives the information you choose to provide and uses it to respond to your request. Avoid sending passenger details or confidential pickup information unless necessary.

Changes
If SignLumo adds cloud services, analytics, or live flight tracking, this statement will be updated to describe the new practices before those features are offered. Check the date above for the latest revision.
